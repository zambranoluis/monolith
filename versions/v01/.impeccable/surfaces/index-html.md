# Common Thread brand presentation

Mode: Experience. Audience: individuals and small teams, plus reviewers of the proposed identity. Visitors understand the workspace idea, inspect a coherent identity, and download usable vector files. Native section navigation and return-to-hub links are the interactions; no scripted motion is required.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Work information becomes useful together. Broad interlocking bands carry that idea across a brand presentation, rather than a generic feature-card landing.

OWN-WORLD: Saturated orange fields, charcoal geometry, cool white pauses, Sora lettering, flat square-edged compositions. A woven M supplies the repeatable geometry.

STORY: Meet Monolith; connect notes, projects, and knowledge; inspect the identity; see it applied; download the assets.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full orange opening. Native navigation above an oversized outlined lowercase wordmark; a large woven symbol beside the concept line and a short workspace explanation below it. The “Explore the identity” link begins the native section journey.

FORM: The user's approved Common Thread plan fixes the identity, six-section order, and code-first method. Seed 7628daad was run; its assigned index 6 and challengers are superseded by the explicit pinned plan. No new choice or comp approval is needed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Review boundary

Desktop and mobile in one batch, then at most one confirmation pass. Inspect wrapping, legibility, keyboard focus, contrast, overflow, actual downloads, local assets, no-JavaScript behavior, and paths under `/monolith/`. Workspace imagery is illustrative. No backend, integration, or publishing. No raster artwork is required: the brand's authored material is reusable vector geometry.
