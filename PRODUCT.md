# Monolith

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web. This repository delivers a static local brand showcase in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Users

Monolith initially serves individuals who already use OpenJM and want to organise their work information. The showcase serves people learning, reviewing, or applying Monolith’s identity.

## Product Purpose

Monolith is a standalone CrimsonTide workspace combining pages and structured records. It makes a person’s work information organised, understandable, and useful as context for OpenJM.

## Positioning

**The Work Index.** A workspace that gives work information a clear place and visible relationships. **Tagline:** Work, in context.

**Product description:** Monolith brings pages and structured records together in one workspace, so your work has a clear place and OpenJM has useful context to read, explain, summarise, and help you plan.

## Journeys, Capabilities, and Scope

- Learn the product purpose, story, and brand architecture.
- Browse six linked chapters: introduction, story, logo, colour and typography, voice and applications, resources.
- Explore a fictional Harbor Library project through overview, records, document, and OpenJM explanation concepts.
- Select either, both, or no example sources; see deterministic example responses and links to their source material.
- Compare light and dark workspace treatments; download the reusable identity.

The workspace and OpenJM connection are product direction confirmed by the implementation plan, not evidence of a shipped product. No database, authentication, storage, synchronisation, live AI, or record-changing mechanism is implemented here. The demonstration sends no information, writes no records, and calls no OpenJM service.

## Operating Context

International English. Local delivery. WCAG 2.2 AA target. Essential content must remain readable without JavaScript. Native scrolling, keyboard access, visible focus, and reduced-motion support are required.

Commercial availability, pricing, permissions, integration contracts, synchronisation, accuracy guarantees, and privacy/security policies are unresolved with the product owner. They block a live product launch, not this local showcase.

## Brand Commitments

Name: **Monolith**, capital M. Endorsement: **By CrimsonTide**, secondary to Monolith. OpenJM is the connected independent product; it is not Monolith’s parent or replacement name. CrimsonTide references predate Monolith and do not establish a current product catalogue.

Personality: **clear, grounded, resourceful, composed**. Voice: straightforward international English, with direct verbs, concrete information, and room for human judgement. Explain selected current workspace information without implying automatic synchronisation, guaranteed accuracy, or authority to change it.

Visual direction: **The Work Index**, bright white surfaces, dark ink, vivid violet indexing; Archivo headings and Source Sans 3 reading/UI. See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) for the implemented visual system and [brand guide](docs/brand-guide.md) for the complete identity.

## Evidence on Hand

The user-supplied implementation plan is the source of confirmed product intent. Reference product documents under `references/` supply historical context, not Monolith launch evidence. Three generated concept images were reviewed before SVG artwork or showcase implementation; they are exploration material, not product screenshots. [Asset provenance](assets/PROVENANCE.json) and adjacent prompt files record origins.

Harbor Library, Maya Ellis, Leo Grant, tasks, dates, and the opening brief are fictional demonstration information, consistently labelled in the interface. There are no customer claims, testimonials, usage metrics, or performance promises.

## Product Principles

1. Give each piece of information a clear place.
2. Preserve the difference between narrative pages and structured records.
3. Keep selected sources visible alongside an explanation.
4. Let the person review the result and decide what to do.
5. State evidence and limits in plain language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Content and downloads work without JavaScript. Interaction uses native checkboxes and buttons with clear names. Responses announce changes politely. Demonstration record tables retain semantic headers and can scroll within a labelled, focusable region. Meaning does not depend on colour. Reduced motion removes animation and smooth scrolling.
