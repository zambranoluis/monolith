# Design

Status: unconfigured starter for projects with user-facing design concerns. Keep this neutral source unconfigured. In an adopted project, preserve established design owners; this root document may be a concise entry pointing to them. Populate only verified facts and settled decisions already authorized within scope. Mark unresolved material items as unknown with an owner and the work they block; remove inapplicable prompts. Observed design is evidence, not automatically intended requirements. Documentation maintenance does not approve a new product or design direction; explicit read-only requests prohibit these writes.

## Direction and sources

Record the confirmed design direction, its sources and decision makers, and applicable constraints. Link detailed owners, surface briefs, assets, and the token source instead of duplicating their rules. Ground the direction in [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md); no palette, style, or skill-specific schema is selected by this starter.

## Colors and typography

Record approved color roles and tokens, typography and font sources, hierarchy, and relevant contrast and readability requirements.

## Layout, elevation, and shapes

Record layout, spacing, content/viewport responsiveness, breakpoints, elevation/depth, and shapes only where established or explicitly decided.

## Components, states, accessibility, and motion

Record component ownership and variants, reachable states, semantic and accessibility requirements, keyboard/touch access, focus behavior, and meaning beyond color. Record relevant motion entry, exit, repositioning, interruption, responsive behavior, and reduced motion. Link detailed specifications and permitted evidence; use [CODE.md](AGENTS/CODE.md#verification) and the lanes in [README.md](README.md#commands-and-verification) to verify applicable behavior.
