Space follows meaning.

Use a 4 px foundation, 8–24 px within related groups, and 48–96 px between ideas. Straight rules and measured alignment do the organising.

Shape follows purpose.

Square artwork. Quiet 4–8 px interface corners. Icons use a consistent 1.5 px stroke on a 24 px grid. Labels make the meaning explicit.

Motion follows attention.

A short sequence brings the index together. State changes take 180 ms. No scroll effects; reduced motion keeps every element still and readable.

Opening brief

Maya Ellis · 2 October 2026 · Demonstration data

Harbor Library will create a welcoming neighbourhood space for reading and small workshops. The planned opening is 18 November 2026.

Before we open

Confirm the shelving layout and prepare a volunteer rota. Start with a small, well-supported opening, then use visitor feedback to plan the first workshops.

What good looks like

Visitors can find a book, ask for help, and feel comfortable staying. The opening plan prioritises a usable space and a prepared volunteer team.
